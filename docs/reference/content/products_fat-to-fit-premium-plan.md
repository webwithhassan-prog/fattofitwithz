Diabetes Control Program

Duration: 3 Months

A structured 3-month program designed to support better blood sugar management by addressing insulin resistance, insulin spikes, and hyperglycemia through personalized nutrition, guided exercise, and consistent monitoring.

Main Benefits:

• Personalized diet plans designed for better blood glucose control
• Practical home-based meals with no fancy or complicated cooking
• Nutrition strategies to support improved insulin sensitivity
• Guidance to minimize unnecessary blood sugar spikes
• Regular follow-ups to monitor progress and make timely adjustments
• Structured workout patterns with professionally guided sessions
• Goal-oriented support for improving HbA1c levels toward a healthier range

Who Is This Program For?

Women dealing with diabetes, insulin resistance, blood sugar fluctuations, or difficulty maintaining healthy glucose levels, who want a structured lifestyle-based approach alongside their medical care.

How the Program Works:

You receive a personalized nutrition plan based on your health needs, lifestyle, and goals, using simple foods that are practical to prepare at home. Regular check-ins help monitor your progress, dietary response, and consistency, while guided workouts support overall fitness and metabolic health.
