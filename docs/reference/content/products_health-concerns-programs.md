Health Concerns Program

Duration: 3 Months

A specialized 3-month program designed to address specific health and nutritional concerns through condition-focused nutrition, structured lifestyle guidance, and personalized fitness support.

Covers:
• Hormonal Imbalance / PCOS / Thyroid
• Menopause
• Metabolic / Fatty Liver / Cholesterol
• Uric Acid

Main Benefits:

• A focused approach based on your specific health condition and individual requirements
• Personalized diet plans with properly divided food groups to meet calorie and nutritional needs
• Balanced nutrition designed to help prevent or address common nutritional deficiencies
• Practical guidance on foods that support your specific health goals
• Structured and guided workout patterns according to your needs and fitness level
• Regular follow-ups to monitor progress and make necessary adjustments

Who Is This Program For?

Women who are dealing with specific health concerns such as hormonal imbalance, PCOS, thyroid concerns, menopause, cholesterol, fatty liver, uric acid issues, or other nutrition-related conditions.

How the Program Works:

Your health concern, lifestyle, dietary preferences, calorie requirements, and nutritional needs are assessed first. A personalized plan is created with appropriate food-group distribution, regular follow-ups, and a suitable workout pattern.
