1. Refund Policy

All program purchases are non-refundable once the program has been activated or access to the purchased content or services has been provided, subject to any rights that may apply under applicable law.

This includes:

- Workout sessions

- Diet plans

- Progress tracking
- Check-ins
- Other services included in the purchased package

2. Change of Mind

Refunds will generally not be provided because a client:
- Changes their mind after purchasing
- Decides not to continue
- Does not use the program
- Does not attend workout sessions
- Does not follow the diet or workout plan
- Does not achieve the expected results
- Becomes too busy to participate

Clients are encouraged to review the program description and included services before purchasing.

3. Duplicate Payments

If a client is accidentally charged more than once for the same purchase, the duplicate transaction may be reviewed and, where verified, the duplicate payment may be refunded.

If a genuine technical issue prevents access to a purchased service, the client should contact:

fattofitwithz@gmail.com

A technical issue does not automatically qualify for a refund if the issue can reasonably be resolved.

5. Freeze Policy

Where a program includes a freeze option, the freeze must be requested according to the terms applicable to that specific package.

A freeze temporarily pauses the client's program access or participation period, where applicable. It does not constitute a refund.

Freeze requests made outside the applicable freeze period or after the program has expired may not be accepted.

6. Cancellation

Once a paid program has been activated or accessed, cancellation does not automatically create an entitlement to a refund.

If a cancellation request is made before access has been activated, it may be reviewed according to the terms of the purchased program and applicable law.

7. Workout Session Changes

If a workout session is postponed, rescheduled or cancelled due to circumstances on our side, an alternative session, recording or reasonable replacement may be provided where applicable.

A change to a particular workout session schedule does not automatically qualify for a refund.

8. Program Results

We do not guarantee specific weight-loss, fat-loss, measurement, fitness or health outcomes.

Failure to achieve a particular result does not qualify for a refund.

9. Exceptional Circumstances

Requests outside this policy may be reviewed individually.

Where required by applicable law, statutory consumer rights will continue to apply.
