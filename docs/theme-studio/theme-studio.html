FAQ

Questions, answered

Do I need a gym membership?

No. Workouts are designed for home: bodyweight, dumbbells or water bottles, and resistance bands.

Do I have to eat special or expensive foods?

No. Plans use everyday desi food, adjusted to your routine and preferences, without extreme restrictions.

Can I join from another country?

Yes. Women from many countries join online, and prices are shown in your local currency.
